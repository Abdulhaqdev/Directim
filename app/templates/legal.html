Privacy Policy

{{ business }} · Last updated: 10 November 2026

{{ business }} uses an automated assistant to answer messages and comments sent to our own Instagram business accounts. This policy explains what data we process and why.

What data we receive

  • Your Instagram username and the Instagram-scoped ID that Meta provides to us.
  • The messages, comments and images you send to our Instagram accounts.
  • Your phone number and city, only if you choose to share them to place an order.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions about our products, prices and delivery.
  • To pass your order request (name, phone number, product) to our sales team so they can contact you.
  • Message text is processed by an AI service (Anthropic Claude) only to generate replies. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Sharing

Your order request is shared only with our own sales staff through a private Telegram group. We do not share your data with other third parties, except the service providers named above, which process it on our behalf.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on our own server. Conversations are kept only as long as needed to answer you and handle your orders. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to see or delete your data at any time. See Data Deletion or contact us at {{ contact }}.
